Cuetsy Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Cuetsy uses the calendar events you select to show upcoming meetings and schedule alarms. Calendar access is optional until you enable meeting alarms. One-off Quick Add reminders do not require calendar access.

Information used by the app

Calendar event details, Quick Add reminder titles and times, alarm preferences, and theme choices are processed on your devices. Cuetsy stores the information needed for its schedule, alarms, and widgets locally on your iPhone and, when paired, shares relevant schedule information with your Apple Watch. Cuetsy does not operate an account or cloud service for this information.

If you use the feature-request form, Cuetsy prepares your message in Mail or the iOS share sheet. You can review it before sending. The message includes the text you enter and the app version; it does not attach calendar data. If you send an email, its contents and your email address are received through your chosen email service.

Analytics and advertising

Cuetsy does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not sell your information.

Other services

When you choose to open a link from a calendar event, Cuetsy hands that link to iOS and the destination app or website. That destination has its own privacy practices. Apple’s Calendar, AlarmKit, Live Activities, and Watch services operate under Apple’s policies.

Your choices

You can change Calendar and alarm permissions in iOS Settings. You can remove saved reminders in Cuetsy. Removing Cuetsy from your devices removes its local app data, subject to any backups controlled by your device settings.
