Cuetsy Support

Cuetsy helps you stay ahead of calendar meetings with alarms and lets you quickly add one-off reminders.

Getting started

Allow Calendar access to see upcoming meetings. Allow Alarms so Cuetsy can alert you. Use Quick Add for a reminder that isn't connected to a calendar event.

Apple Watch

Install Cuetsy on your paired Apple Watch, then add its complication from the Watch app or your watch face editor.

Contact
