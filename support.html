Cuetsy Support

Cuetsy shows upcoming calendar events and lets you create meeting and one-off alarms. To enable meeting alarms, allow Calendar and Alarms access when Cuetsy asks. You can manage these permissions later in iOS Settings.

For help, report an issue, or request a feature, email sethpaxton@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS/watchOS version, and what happened. Please avoid sending private calendar details unless they are needed to explain the problem.
