UpSoon

See what’s next. Feel time passing. UpSoon helps you prepare for calendar meetings and create quick reminders on iPhone, with Apple Watch and Mac companions.

Get started

For meeting alarms, allow Calendar and Alarms access and select calendars in Settings. Choose how early alarms ring. The dashboard shows a countdown, room or call link; Coming up shows your schedule. Add an optional prep or wrap-up alarm when you need more time.

Quick reminders

Tap Reminder to create a one-off or repeating reminder without creating a calendar event. Calendar access is not needed for Quick Add. Alarm permission is needed for alarm delivery.

Apple Watch and Mac

The paired Watch shows the schedule and can request a reminder while the iPhone is reachable. Widgets and Live Activities are optional. The Mac menu-bar app provides local timers and meeting alerts while it is running; Mac timers and preferences do not sync with iPhone.

Troubleshooting

If a meeting is missing, check Calendar access and the calendar selection, then open UpSoon to refresh. Background refresh is controlled by iOS. Check alarm permissions and your chosen alarm type if an alert is unexpected. On Watch, keep the paired iPhone reachable for reminder requests.
